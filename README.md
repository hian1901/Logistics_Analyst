## 🔍 2. Core Operational Insights
### 👨‍✈️ Drivers (Workforce & SLA Compliance)
* **Tenure Dictates Rating:** Driver rating scales directly with experience — 1st-year drivers average **2.50 stars**, whereas seasoned drivers (7–10 years) achieve **4.33–4.67 stars**.
* **Concentrated Delay Risk:** 5 drivers consistently delay > 23% of their routes (led by *Lisa Moore at 25.66%* and *Patricia Martinez at 24.83%*).
* 👉 **Action:** Launch a 1-on-1 Senior Mentorship program for new hires; review route difficulty and congestion factors for high-delay drivers.
### 🏢 Regional Hubs (Capacity & Bottlenecks)
* **Dallas Overcapacity:** Dallas Main Hub handled **7,345 orders (26.3% of total volume)** against an intake capacity of only 250 orders/batch; in contrast, Houston Hub operates at capacity 380 but handled only 6,875 orders.
* **El Paso Operational Excellence:** El Paso leads network performance with the **highest OTIF (80.57%)** and **shortest Lead Time (36.96 hrs)**.
* 👉 **Action:** Implement dynamic load-balancing to re-route 15% of Dallas inbound volume to adjacent hubs; standardize hub intake SOPs using El Paso as a benchmark.
### 🚛 Vehicles (Fleet Health & Reliability)
* **Maintenance Hotspots:** Out of 537 fleet-wide breakdowns, the **Freightliner M2 caused 153 incidents (28.5%)**, benching 3 of 9 units. The **Ford F-150** suffered the highest failure rate per delivery (**3.46%**).
* **Hero Fleet Model:** **Mercedes Sprinter** maintained **100% fleet availability (9/9 active)**, handling 5,082 orders with a minimal 1.81% breakdown rate.
* 👉 **Action:** Shift Freightliner M2 units to a strict 30-day **Preventive Maintenance** schedule; prioritize Mercedes Sprinter for future fleet procurement.
